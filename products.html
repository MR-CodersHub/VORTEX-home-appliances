Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What warranty is included with appliance purchases?
All VORTEX appliances come with standard 2 to 5 years manufacturer warranty, plus a 10-Year Inverter Compressor/Motor guarantee on Samsung, LG, Bosch, and Whirlpool models.
Does VORTEX offer old appliance trade-ins?
Yes! We provide instant trade-in valuations up to $250 for working or non-working old refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners during showroom enquiry, purchase consultation, or delivery scheduling.
How quickly can my appliance be delivered & installed?
Same-day and next-day delivery slots are available in major metropolitan areas. Our team will contact you within 2 hours of order confirmation to lock in your preferred delivery time.
Can I inspect the appliance in person before buying?
Absolutely! Visit our main VORTEX showroom or request a live 1-on-1 virtual video walkthrough with an appliance specialist.
