Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At VORTEX Home Appliances & Care Inc. ("VORTEX", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share with us through our showroom visits, online appliance purchases, repair bookings, warranty registrations, and digital services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to process appliance orders, dispatch certified technicians to your home, validate OEM warranty coverage, and maintain preventative AMC care schedules:

  • Contact Information: Full name, service address, email address, and phone number for dispatch alerts.
  • Appliance Records: Brand, model numbers, serial numbers, date of purchase, and diagnostic history.
  • Payment Data: Payment card details processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (we never store raw CVV codes).
  • Technical Diagnostics: Wi-Fi connected smart appliance error logs submitted for diagnostic troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business and service operations:

  • Fulfilling white-glove delivery, unboxing, and OEM installation appointments.
  • Dispatching factory-certified technicians with the correct genuine replacement parts.
  • Sending warranty expiration alerts, recall notices, and preventative maintenance reminders.
  • Processing VIP Club discounts and promotional rebates when opted in.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Information is only shared with authorized manufacturers (Samsung, LG, Bosch, Miele, Daikin) solely for verifying OEM warranty coverage, and trusted logistics couriers for delivery scheduling.

4. Data Protection & Security Standards

We employ 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption across all showroom enquiry portals and repair scheduling databases. Our master technicians operate under strict non-disclosure protocols during in-home visits.

5. Your Data Rights & Inquiries

You may request an export of your appliance service records or ask for deletion of your profile by emailing privacy@vortex.com or calling our hotline at +1 (800) 555-VORTEX.
